Setup & FAQ

What do I need?

Desktop Microsoft Excel for Windows: Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365. The journal uses one small macro (it highlights the row you are on), so it does not run in Excel for Mac, Excel Online, the mobile apps or Google Sheets. Download the free demo first: if the demo works on your computer, the full journal will too.

First time you open it (important)

  1. Save the file to your computer, then right-click it → Properties.
  2. At the bottom of the General tab tick Unblock, then click OK. Windows blocks macros in every file downloaded from the internet, so without this step the automation stays off.
  3. Open the file. If a yellow bar appears, click Enable Content.
  4. Set your starting balance, commission and time zone at the top of the Journal sheet, then enter your first trade.

Do I need to type the lot size or direction?

No. Enter entry, stop loss and take profits, pick the outcome and risk from the dropdowns; Long/Short, lot size, P/L and R:R are calculated. Instrument contract sizes and pip values are editable on the Markets sheet.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. It is a normal Excel file on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Something does not work?

Email patterntradersltd@gmail.com with a screenshot. Most problems are fixed by the Unblock step above. The Premium journal comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
