Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

Apex Trading Tools (APXTT) is run by Mátyás Stverteczky, Hungary. Contact for any privacy question or request: stvertmatyi@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

This website sets no cookies of its own and has no advertising trackers. The tools run entirely in your browser: your trade files and the numbers you type into Edge Check or the Lot Size Calculator are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else. Edge Check keeps a small counter of unlocked reports in your browser's local storage, the calculator keeps the day's exchange rates there, and the feedback poll remembers that you answered it; none of this leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

Anonymous visit statistics

Since 29 October 2026 the pages count visits with GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly statistics service. It uses no cookies, does not track you across sites and does not build a profile of you. What it records is aggregate: which page was viewed, the referring site, the browser, the screen size and the country (derived from the request), and how often some actions happen (for example: a lot size was calculated, an Edge Check report was run, a poll option was chosen, a link to Gumroad was clicked). It never receives what you type, your trade files, file names or results. GoatCounter processes this on our behalf; see its privacy policy and GDPR information. We use the counts only to see which tools are used and what to improve. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregate, how the site is used. A content blocker that blocks GoatCounter does not affect any tool.

Questions and requests

The "Ask a question or request a tool" box sends what you write there, the name of the page, and an email address only if you add one, to our Google Form (Google, as our processor). Nothing is sent until you press Send. We use it only to answer you or to build the tool you asked for, and we delete your message on request (write to the address above). The one-tap poll next to it sends no text: only an anonymous count of the option chosen, through GoatCounter.

What we do collect, and why

  • Purchases and free products (Gumroad). When you buy or get a free product (the journal, the free demo, the Edge Check unlock code, the waitlist), Gumroad processes your email and payment details as our payment and delivery provider. We see your email, the product and the date. Legal basis: performing the contract (delivering what you asked for).
  • Emails. We use your email to deliver the product and for how-to messages about it. We send product news only if you subscribe to it separately; you can unsubscribe from any email, or write to the address above.
  • Edge Check license keys. If you use a report key, only the key and our product id are sent to Gumroad to check it.
  • Exchange rates (Lot Size Calculator). The calculator page asks frankfurter.dev for the day's ECB reference exchange rates. Nothing you type is sent; like any web request, that service can see your IP address. The rates are kept in your browser's local storage for 12 hours.
  • Visit statistics and feedback. Anonymous visit and click counts (GoatCounter) and anything you send through the question box (Google Form), as described above.
  • Tutorial video (home page). The video is embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When the video area loads, your browser contacts YouTube/Google, which can see your IP address and apply its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as accounting law requires; email contacts until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Messages sent through the question box are kept until they are answered or the tool is built, and deleted earlier on request. Visit statistics are kept as aggregate counts; GoatCounter does not store your IP address or a lasting identifier for you (see its privacy policy).

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, and you can object or withdraw consent at any time, by writing to stvertmatyi@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, naih.hu).

Processors

Gumroad, Inc. (sales, delivery, emails, license keys), GitHub, Inc. (hosting), GoatCounter (anonymous visit statistics) and Google (the question box's form). The embedded YouTube video is Google's own service.
